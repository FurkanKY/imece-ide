# Code of Conduct

Participate respectfully and constructively. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, targeted disruption, and sharing another person's private information
without consent are not acceptable. Keep criticism focused on ideas and work;
respect boundaries and follow maintainers' reasonable requests to stop harmful
behavior.

For content that violates GitHub's terms or needs platform-level attention, use
GitHub's [report content / abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)
and its available report or block tools. Do not use security vulnerability
advisories for ordinary conduct concerns. No private conduct mailbox or response
time is offered here.
